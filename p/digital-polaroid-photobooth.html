📸 About Digital Polaroid Photo Booth

Love the aesthetic nostalgia of instant film cameras? Our Digital Polaroid Photo Booth combines the spontaneous, tactile feel of vintage instant film with the convenience of modern browser technology.

Clean white borders and bottom caption space styled after iconic square and mini instant film prints.
