📸 About 3-Photo Strip Maker Online

The 3-photo vertical strip is the gold standard of arcade and carnival photo booths. Our 3-Photo Strip Maker lets you capture three sequential portraits and automatically compiles them into an aesthetic, balanced vertical card with custom branding and date stamps.

✨ Golden Ratio Proportions

Engineered with balanced padding, margins, and gaps for optimal visual aesthetics.

✨ Live 3-Shot Sequence Counter

Interactive badge displays your 0/3, 1/3, 2/3 progress so you always know your sequence.

✨ Print Ready Resolution

Generates crisp high-density pixels formatted for standard 2x6 and 4x6 photo paper prints.

✨ Instant Retake Button

Don't love a shot? Hit 'Retake' to reset and start a fresh 3-pose session in seconds.

💡 Pro Ideas & Tips for Classic 3-Cut

  • Tell a micro-story across the 3 frames (e.g. looking left, looking center, looking right).
  • Print multiple strips on a single 4x6 sheet and slice them down the middle with scissors.
  • Use them as personalized bookmarks for your favorite books and journals.
