Privacy Policy & Data Protection
Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Immediately

Privacy-First Philosophy: 100% Local & Private

At Photobooth Studio, your privacy is our foundational architectural principle. Unlike conventional cloud apps that upload your photos to remote servers, our application is engineered to operate entirely inside your web browser.

Zero Cloud Photo Storage:

Your webcam stream, captured poses, customized headlines, and exported photo strips are processed strictly in your device's local memory (RAM). No pictures are ever sent over the internet, stored on external databases, or viewed by anyone but you.

What Information We Handle

Camera & Photo Feed

Processed 100% locally. We access your webcam solely via the standard browser MediaStream API to show the live viewfinder and snap poses onto an HTML5 canvas. No media files leave your computer or smartphone.

Personal Accounts

Zero registration required. You do not need to sign up, sign in, or provide an email, phone number, name, password, or credit card. Photobooth Studio is completely frictionless and anonymous.

Biometric Data

No biometric profiling. We do not perform facial recognition, iris scanning, identity mapping, or emotional classification. Visual styling filters are standard client-side mathematical shaders.

Server Logs

Minimal delivery metrics. Like all websites, our static hosting platform (Vercel) records standard non-identifiable technical requests (HTTP status, general country, user-agent) strictly for DDoS prevention.

Detailed Privacy Provisions

1. Camera Hardware Permissions

When you access Photobooth Studio, your browser will ask for explicit permission to access your webcam or front/back camera. You can grant or deny access at your discretion.

  • The camera feed is actively engaged only while the browser tab is open and visible.
  • Closing the tab or clicking camera permissions in your browser URL bar instantly releases hardware access.
  • Audio/microphone permissions are never requested because Photobooth Studio produces silent photographic strips.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

Photobooth Studio does not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or fingerprinting scripts.

  • Session Memory: Your customized headlines (e.g., Star Name, Event Title) remain in local memory for the active session so you can easily retake poses.
  • No Retargeting: We do not track your browsing activities across other websites or sell behavioral information to ad networks.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure

To deliver a fast, reliable, and aesthetically pleasing experience, we utilize reputable infrastructure providers:

  • Vercel: Hosts our static web assets (HTML, CSS, JS) with global edge caching and DDoS mitigation.
  • Google Fonts: Delivers typography stylesheets (Outfit, Bebas Neue, Chewy, Playfair Display) via optimized Google CDN endpoints.
  • Weboraco: Software development partner powering the web application architecture.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Because Photobooth Studio does not gather, store, or transmit personal data or photographs, our service is inherently safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. We encourage parents and teachers to enjoy fun retro photobooth creations with peace of mind.

5. Changes to This Policy

Any revisions to our privacy practices will be posted directly to this page with an updated timestamp. Because our fundamental model relies on zero server-side storage, our commitment to your privacy remains unchanged.

6. Contact & Questions

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices or security implementation, please reach out via our official website or visit our partner at Weboraco.
